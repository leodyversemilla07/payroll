# Payroll release gates — NOT cleared for live payroll

This is a verification checklist, **not** legal, tax, or bank-format approval. Implemented workflows and passing tests do not constitute independent regulatory validation. Do not use Payroll for live payroll or treat its worksheet/snapshot as a validated official BIR Form 2316.

## Implemented development capabilities (not release approval)

- Biometric CSV timestamps without offsets are interpreted as Manila wall time; explicit offsets preserve instants, and grouping/shift/night-window comparisons use Manila dates without host-local Date operations. Strict CSV date/type checks and ambiguous/overnight punch flags fail closed. Imports create unapproved DTRs, refuse to replace existing attendance, and commit the full API batch/import audit in one serializable transaction. Half-day payroll reconciles a paid 240-minute credit against either consistent full-shift or already-adjusted deductions, preserving genuine remaining shortfalls and the source DTR; ambiguous break/slot/deduction totals block payroll. Regression tests cover host timezones, stored imports/rollback and monthly/daily/hourly payslips. This does not validate AM/PM leave slots, overnight pairing, break allocation, holiday scheduling or shift-policy entitlement.

- Regular payroll honors `PER_CUTOFF` versus `MONTHLY` allowance amounts, splitting monthly grants before tax/de minimis classification and preserving odd centavos in the second cutoff. Unknown frequencies, negative grants, missing de minimis categories and ambiguous monthly allowance partial periods fail closed. Active positive-balance loan eligibility uses inclusive repayment civil dates at cutoff end; future and expired loans have no collection entries, expired balances remain outstanding, and invalid active profiles block payroll. The loan-creation API rejects inverted date ranges. PostgreSQL regressions cover both cutoff allowance totals, replay/cancellation/recalculation and loan date boundaries/refunds. These scheduling conventions still require contract/company-policy approval; no missing-cutoff allowance true-up, versioned allowance policy, partial-period proration or bank payment is implied.

- Regular payroll excludes future hires and limits daily/hourly attendance and paid-leave credits to the post-hire part of the cutoff. Monthly mid-cutoff hires and nonstandard monthly periods are blocked pending an approved proration policy. Every weekday under the existing Monday–Friday model requires an explicit approved DTR or approved full-day paid leave; missing dates, zero-work non-absence placeholders, and missing/zero-work half-day DTRs fail closed. PostgreSQL regressions verify no payroll/payslip/loan/audit side effects on rejection, while the real-browser workflow verifies a sparse cutoff is rejected and can be retried after its missing DTR is recorded through the UI, with an approved paid half day removing rather than duplicating the source DTR's undertime. This does not validate rotating/weekend schedules, holiday entitlement, new-hire statutory contribution policy, or a configurable half-day shift/break calendar.

- A separate real-browser integration suite now operates the production-built web UI against the actual API and isolated PostgreSQL, without mocked responses. It verifies synthetic onboarding, explicit attendance writes/Manila punch conversion, leave allocation and approval/debit, distinct payroll maker/checker accounts, payslip reads, persisted disbursement status after reload, transactional audit records and signed-in tax correction requester/reviewer separation with persisted version history. Only local disposable test databases with explicit opt-in are accepted. This happy-path integration test does not validate every payroll calculation, employment/holiday schedule, final-pay/tax scenario or a bank payment; outstanding correctness and external release gates remain open.

- Payroll-run API creation (regular and 13th-month), approval, disbursement marking and cancellation commit their audit records with the payroll transaction. Conditional, serializable lifecycle transitions protect approval provenance and prevent duplicate disbursement transitions/refunds. Creation replays do not duplicate audits or loan collections; cancellation audits retain the removed run/payslip snapshot. `DISBURSED` is a recorded status, not bank-transfer acknowledgment. These transaction safeguards alone do not establish employment/schedule completeness, approved allowance/repayment policies, or regulatory correctness; outstanding payroll/compliance validation gaps remain open.

- `compliance.listYearEndReconciliations` returns a read-only, tenant-scoped review queue for employees with disbursed payslips paid in the selected year (2023–2100). `BLOCKED_REVIEW` rows have null financial figures, not zero. Classified totals exclude blocked employees and are **not filing totals**.
- Prior-employer declarations, transcribed income, and monthly remittance references are recorded with actor/time provenance. Entered amounts and references are **self-reported, unverified**, not proof of filing, remittance, or document authenticity. Missing/conflicting evidence and missing/mismatched remittance amounts block reconciliation.
- Previous-employer taxable income and withholding are incorporated in annualization with present-employer payroll. Prior amounts are taken as transcribed, not reclassified. Minimum-wage earners with prior taxable income remain blocked for manual review.
- Evidence/settlement correction APIs create pending proposals with recorded/proposed values; they do not alter effective evidence. A different signed-in, active HR/admin approves or rejects with a reason; caller-supplied reviewer identities are rejected. Approval publishes one higher version and attributes review to the authenticated actor. Self-review, unauthorized/inactive/demoted/foreign reviewers, duplicate pending targets, replay/concurrent duplicate decisions and changed proposal sources fail closed. Settlement review exposes the live computed difference and rechecks the frozen payroll/evidence source fingerprint. Recording, proposal/review and settlement APIs audit within serializable transactions. PostgreSQL and real-browser regressions verify signed-in review, rollback and retained history. This still is not durable source-document storage or proof that the reviewer inspected authentic supporting evidence.
- `compliance.recordYearEndSettlement` records an attestation of deficiency withheld/refund paid with disbursed payroll. Active employees settle with December pay; separated employees settle with termination-month pay. Deficiencies above the month's disbursed net pay are rejected. Reconciliation recomputes remaining balances after later payments/evidence changes. This record does not transfer money or independently verify the stated payment.
- `compliance.issueBir2316` records an HR-controlled, versioned issuance snapshot after a conclusive, fully settled reconciliation and refuses pending employee/remittance corrections. Issuance/void and audits share serializable transactions. Source fingerprints cover employee/employer data, compensation, tax-year disbursed payroll and evidence/settlement versions; reads and the review queue label changed sources STALE and legacy snapshots UNVERIFIABLE. CURRENT only means sources match, not that calculations or official fields are approved. Frozen snapshots are never silently refreshed; void/re-issue preserves history, and conservative source changes may require review. The deadline remains informational and termination issuance timing is not resolved. The snapshot/worksheet is **not a validated official form**, filing acknowledgment, or substituted-filing eligibility verdict.

- Leave management reads recorded balances without granting default credits, files explicit requests, and atomically audits paid approval/debit or pending rejection. Self-approval, overlapping approvals and processed regular cutoffs are blocked. HR-controlled credit allocation/adjustments require explicit amounts, revision, source and reason; balance, immutable API history and audit commit together without resetting usage. Pending withdrawal and HR-controlled approved reversal preserve decision history and never refund twice. Paid reversals require the original recorded debit; legacy approvals without provenance require manual reconciliation. Processed regular cutoffs and linked-self refunds are blocked, balance/request audits commit together, and cancellation never alters DTRs or payslips. References are attestations, not authenticated source documents. Recorded credits (including legacy defaults), leave eligibility, holiday treatment and nonstandard schedules still require independent policy/statutory verification; this workflow does not automatically provision or certify entitlements.

- `employees.calculateFinalPay` is an HR/super-admin read-only exit-year preview, not a posting or payment workflow. Explicit scenario days, recorded SIL availability, prior monetization history, reviewed retirement classification/claim history and proposed authorized debt replace guessed defaults. A repeatable-read snapshot includes effective prior-employer documents and the latest signed tax settlement so refunds/collections are not proposed twice. Pending source corrections and employee payroll reservations block preview; unknown MWE classification, early/private retirement and inconsistent sources fail closed. Signed shortfalls/unfunded proposed deductions remain visible and no balances, payroll or audits are changed by the query. Current compensation is not an effective-dated historical rate snapshot. Source references, user statements and recorded balances are attestations, not proof of entitlement or lawful collection. The configured statutory scope, leave/benefit caps, prior-year arrears, external usage, insufficient-pay follow-up and actual settlement remain independent release gates.
- Periodic and annual withholding now use integer centavos with final half-up rounding; leave split arithmetic conserves the computed cash amount. Regression tests check boundaries, explicit history, settlement offsets and shortfalls. This is arithmetic verification, not statutory/rate/classification approval; odd-salary and split-contribution conservation and other whole-codebase findings still require stabilization.

## Primary references inspected

- [BIR RR 11-2018, §2.79(B)(5)(b)](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/Digest%20RR%2011-2018.pdf): annualized year-end and termination tax, prior employers, final-pay deduction and refund timing. The 2023-onwards annual table is in this regulation; its [Annex E](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/Annex%20E%20RR%2011-2018.pdf) is the periodic withholding table.
- [BIR Form 2316 (September 2021 ENCS)](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/2316%20Sep%202021%20ENCS_Final_corrected.pdf) and [BIR's forms page](https://www.bir.gov.ph/bir-forms?tab=Certificates&idTag=BIR2316&datasetCode=3381&label=2316&type=TAB%20LINK): the actual form's summary is items **19–28**, not the formerly printed worksheet lines 25–32. Previous-employer fields, tax credits, signatures, and the full official layout require validation. The forms page describes issuance by January 31 or on termination; verify the latest form/version and applicable regulations before issuing.
- [RR 11-2018 §2.83.4](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/Digest%20RR%2011-2018.pdf): substituted filing requires only one employer and correctly withheld tax. Matching calculated tax and payslip withholding alone cannot prove eligibility.

## Required reconciliation before BIR 2316 or 1604-C

All release gates below remain **open**:

- [ ] Independently sign off 2026 rules/thresholds (including later amendments), de minimis classification, statutory contribution rates, benefits/minimum-wage earnings, and tax fixtures with a tax professional. Unit tests validate arithmetic, not regulatory approval.
- [ ] Verify recorded leave credits and their provenance, statutory/company eligibility, supported work schedules, public holidays, half-day treatment, cancellation/debit provenance and paid-leave/DTR reconciliation. Reading or approving a recorded balance is not proof of entitlement; legacy default allocations must not be treated as independently verified.
- [ ] Verify previous-employer 2316 figures, monthly remittance references, and no-previous-employer declarations against source documents/BIR acknowledgments. Validate prior-employer annualization and all applicable tax credits. Reconcile monthly 1601-C, annual 1604-C, and each employee's adjusted withholding; do not infer remittance from payslips or entered references.
- [ ] Add access-controlled, durable source-document storage and verify the authenticated correction/reviewer workflow against supporting evidence. A reference string and a signed-in second reviewer are not themselves document verification.
- [ ] Independently reconcile recorded settlements against actual withholding/refund payments. Validate December/termination timing, insufficient final pay, follow-up collection/refund handling, and later evidence corrections; do not certify a computed-but-unpaid refund.
- [ ] Validate the current official BIR 2316 field mapping, tax credits, signatures, and tax-preparer-reviewed examples. Review snapshot issuance, void/re-issue, stale-evidence handling, access, and audit controls before issuing any official form. No substituted-filing eligibility is inferred.
- [ ] Reconcile 13th-month taxable excess, the shared ₱90,000 bucket, MWE taxable extras, and de minimis excess. Uncertain classifications remain blocked for manual review.

## Bank-file gate (separate from tax)

- [ ] Obtain **current, bank-supplied** file layouts and a test/sandbox upload process for each bank, product, account type, and settlement channel. Existing CSV headers are illustrative, **not verified bank specifications**.
- [ ] Compare sample outputs against official encoding, account formats, field order/width, header/trailer and control totals, decimal handling, rejection feedback, cutoff timing, and duplicate-file behavior.
